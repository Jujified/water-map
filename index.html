About this map

Global topography and bathymetry from ETOPO 2022 (60 arc-second, NOAA NCEI), drawn in the Spilhaus projection: an oblique Adams World in a Square II that puts the connected world ocean in the middle and cuts through the continents instead.

This is a bathtub model. Water fills every cell whose lowest connection to the ocean is below the chosen level. It ignores isostatic rebound, changes to the ice sheets themselves, erosion, sedimentation and tides. Present-day lakes and inland seas, including the Caspian, are always water. Dry land below sea level, such as the Caspian Depression or Death Valley, stays dry until the rising sea can spill into it. Below 0 m the map uses raw elevation.

Realistic tints land by height above the current water level, with hillshade and a 15° graticule. Abstract draws about a million dots, each standing for the same area of Earth. A dot's size follows how much of the planet lies at its elevation, so abyssal plains and lowlands get the biggest dots, and peaks and trenches the smallest.

Drag to pan. Scroll, pinch or use the + / − buttons to zoom, and the reset button (or 0) to fit the whole map.
